🟦 PHASE 2 — AUDIO PROCESSING 
 
Step 2: Extract Audio from Full Match Video 
Tool: FFmpeg 
Command: 
ffmpeg -i match_01_full.mp4 -vn -acodec pcm_s16le -ar 16000 -ac 1 match_01.wav 
Output: 
match_01.wav 
This contains commentary audio. 
 
Step 3: Convert Speech to Text 
Tool: Whisper 
Command: 
whisper match_01.wav --model base 
Output: 
[ 
{"start":10.2,"end":13.5,"text":"advantage being played"}, 
{"start":13.5,"end":16.9,"text":"he goes wide"}, 
{"start":16.9,"end":19.1,"text":"he's over in the corner"} 
] 
Now you know: 
●​ what was spoken 
●​ exact times

🎓 Final Supervisor-Level Explanation 
Full-match rugby videos are transcribed using Whisper with timestamps. The 
transcripts are segmented into overlapping chunks and manually labeled using 
official highlight videos as ground truth. A RoBERTa model is fine-tuned on this 
labeled dataset to learn contextual commentary semantics. In parallel, a rugby 
lexicon extracts domain-specific event cues such as scoring terms, referee 
decisions, and set-piece terminology. Both outputs are fused in a weighted hybrid 
scoring model to generate highlight confidence scores. Consecutive positive chunks 
are merged to produce final highlight timestamps.
